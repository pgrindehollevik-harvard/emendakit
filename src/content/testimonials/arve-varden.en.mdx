Helse Førde has procured the anaphylaxis kit for the entire health trust. The kit is clearly organised and well laid out. Healthcare staff find that the medicines needed in an emergency are easily accessible, and that they can get hold of the medicines they need, when they need them, without having to think about what is required. It therefore feels safe to have the kit available in situations where anaphylaxis may occur. Patients express that this is a reassurance for them too.

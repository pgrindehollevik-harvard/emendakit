## The dental clinic's needs

Anaphylaxis can occur in a dental clinic as a result of various substances used
in treatment, such as local anaesthesia, latex and other materials. EmendaKit
is built on the same robust kit and systematic design as EmendaKit Anaphylaxis
– Healthcare, but with a flowchart adapted to dental services. The flowchart
is shaped for situations in the dental clinic, with a clear focus on rapid
identification and immediate treatment with adrenaline. In a situation where
it may take time before an ambulance arrives, the right equipment and
structure, available immediately, is critical.

## Prepared for the treatment room

The kit is developed for use directly in the treatment room, with quick access
to equipment and a clear overview when it matters. Everything is organised to
support rapid and correct action in an acute situation.

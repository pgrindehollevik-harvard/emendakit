EmendaKit has been developed in close collaboration with Erik Uthus and Fosen Tools, a Norwegian manufacturer with broad experience in custom solutions, tool control and product development. Fosen Tools has its own development department and production, and is known for high quality, precision and innovation.

Through professional expertise, creative problem-solving and a strong focus on quality, Erik Uthus and the team at Fosen Tools have been decisive in turning the idea behind EmendaKit into a robust, professional product ready for use.

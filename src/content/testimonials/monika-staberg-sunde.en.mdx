In the oncology outpatient clinic and the oncology ward we administer many types of medication and chemotherapy that can cause anaphylaxis. We have an anaphylaxis kit in every treatment room in the outpatient clinic, and staff bring the kit into the room when giving treatment on the ward. When a patient has an allergic reaction it is important to have these medicines close to the patient. It is also a great advantage that the kits across Helse Førde have identical contents, so that we have one shared anaphylaxis procedure for the whole health trust.

We use the kit and its overview of which medicines to give when teaching both nurses and doctors in the outpatient clinic and on the ward. Sometimes we have to call the emergency team, and they then also know which medicines we have given before they arrive. A dedicated anaphylaxis procedure and flowchart has also been created in the quality manual.

## Thoughtful design

The kit is built to give clinicians a quick overview and confident handling in
acute situations. The transparent shell makes the contents visible before the
kit is even opened. Inside, fitted compartments give every component a fixed
place, and the adrenaline sits behind a light-protective cover to preserve
quality and shelf life.

## Guidance and training

The national anaphylaxis flowchart is printed on the back of the kit, ready to
hand when a situation requires it. A QR field on the side gives direct access
to short training films that support correct use and add confidence under
pressure.

## Structure that supports the right action

The kit is prepared to a consistent structure where every component has a place
and a purpose. That structure gives a quick overview and supports safe, correct
handling of anaphylaxis.

In 2023 Helse Førde replaced all of its older anaphylaxis kits at the hospitals in Førde, Nordfjordeid and Lærdal with the standardised kit developed in the project "New anaphylaxis kits for Helse Førde". 120 kits were procured, and the health trust maintains its own information page about the kit for staff.

## Thoughtful design

The transparent lid makes the contents visible before the kit is opened.
Inside, every component has a fixed place in a fitted insert, and the
adrenaline is stored away from light.

## Guidance and training

The national anaphylaxis flowchart is printed on the back of the kit. The QR
code on the side opens two short training films.

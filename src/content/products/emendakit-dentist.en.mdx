## The dental clinic's needs

Anaphylaxis in a dental clinic is most often triggered by local anaesthesia,
latex or other materials. The kit is the same as EmendaKit Anaphylaxis
Healthcare, with a flowchart adapted to dental services: rapid identification
and immediate treatment with adrenaline, in the time before an ambulance
arrives.

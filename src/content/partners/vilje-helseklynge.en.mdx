EmendaKit AS is a partner in Vilje Helseklynge, a knowledge and industry cluster that brings together healthcare providers, education, industry and innovation communities to develop the health services of the future.

Membership gives EmendaKit access to professional networks, meeting places and collaborations that contribute to innovation, quality and value creation in healthcare.
